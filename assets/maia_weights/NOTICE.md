# Origin of the Maia weights

The files `maia-1100.pb.gz` through `maia-1900.pb.gz` in this directory come,
unmodified, from the official `v1.0` release of the
[CSSLab/maia-chess](https://github.com/CSSLab/maia-chess) repository:

```
https://github.com/CSSLab/maia-chess/releases/download/v1.0/maia-<rating>.pb.gz
```

License of the source project: GPL-3.0. These weights are redistributed
unmodified, with origin attribution recorded in this file.

Citation (Maia Chess, KDD 2020):

> McIlroy-Young, R., Sen, S., Kleinberg, J., & Anderson, A. (2020).
> Aligning Superhuman AI with Human Behavior: Chess as a Model System.
> Proceedings of the 26th ACM SIGKDD International Conference on Knowledge
> Discovery & Data Mining.
